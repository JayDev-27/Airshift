Airshift — Urban air quality assistant

Airshift turns a city's PM₂.₅ forecast into a practical plan for your day. Choose one of 14 Indian cities, describe your household and schedule, and get the cleanest hour for outdoor activity, steps for cleaner indoor air, lower-emission trip options, and a briefing to share with your school or community.

Follow-up questions are interpreted with Google Gemini structured output, and every recommendation shows the source and assumptions behind it.
